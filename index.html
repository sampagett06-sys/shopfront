What we check

Faults that cost you customers, not points on a scorecard

We only report things that plausibly lose you business, and we show you the evidence for each one so you can check our working.

01

The security warning

Without a certificate, every browser puts "Not Secure" beside your address, and warns again when someone types into a form.

Seen before a word is read
02

Unusable on a phone

The desktop layout renders zoomed out. Visitors pinch, drag sideways to read a sentence, and most give up.

Most local searches are on a phone
03

A number nobody can tap

If the phone number isn't a link, a mobile visitor has to memorise it, leave your site and retype it into the dialler.

The most expensive fault we find
04

Slow to load

Past two seconds people start leaving. Past five, most already have — and Google counts it against you as well.

Compounds on mobile data
05

Missing from Google's local listings

Without structured data your hours, address and reviews don't appear in results. Competitors get a listing twice the size.

You're a plain blue link
06

Dead links and stale footers

Broken pages and a copyright line from three years ago read as "this business may not exist anymore".

Quietly kills trust
